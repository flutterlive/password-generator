What is a password generator?

A random password creator that does the hard part for you.

A password generator is a free online tool that creates strong, random and secure passwords using cryptographic randomness — not predictable words, birthdays or reused logins. Humans are bad at inventing randomness; we pick patterns attackers guess first. This secure password generator picks every character at random from the sets you choose, so the result has no pattern to exploit.

Use it for email, banking, social media, Wi-Fi, work accounts and password managers — anywhere a weak or reused password puts you at risk. Password Generator — FAQ

Quick answers about strong passwords, randomness, length, symbols, PINs and privacy.

Are these passwords really random?

Yes. Characters are picked with your browser's cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) — the same source operating systems use for security keys — not with Math.random.

Is my password sent anywhere?

No. Generation happens entirely on this page, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and the recent-passwords list lives only in this tab's memory.

How long should my password be?

At least 16 characters for everyday accounts, and 20+ for email, banking and anything tied to money or identity. Length is the single biggest factor in crack time.

How is crack time calculated?

From the password's entropy: pool size raised to the length gives the total combinations, divided by 10 billion guesses per second — a fast offline attack. Real online attacks are far slower because sites rate-limit logins.

What makes a password strong?

Three things: length (16+ characters), true randomness (no words or patterns), and a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols. Uniqueness matters too — one password per account, stored in a password manager.

Should I include symbols in my password?

Yes, whenever the site allows them. Symbols grow the character pool from 62 to around 90+, which multiplies the total combinations and pushes crack time from years to centuries at the same length.

Can I generate a PIN or easy-to-type password?

Yes. Pick the PIN preset for a 6-digit numeric code, or Easy to Type for a 14-character password without confusing characters like 0/O and 1/l/I — handy for Wi-Fi or shared devices.

Is this password generator free to use?

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up, no download and no limit. Generate as many strong passwords as you need, on phone or desktop.

What is the best password generator?

The safest one uses cryptographic randomness, lets you control length and character types, shows strength and entropy, and never sends your password to a server. This tool does all four — everything runs locally in your browser.

Should I change my passwords regularly?

Change a password immediately if a site reports a breach or you suspect reuse. Otherwise, a long, unique, random password with two-factor authentication enabled does not need frequent rotation.

How do I remember strong passwords?

You don't need to — use a reputable password manager to store each unique password, and protect the manager itself with a long master password and 2FA.

Why use a strong password generator?

Most account takeovers start with a weak, reused or leaked password. A dedicated random password generator removes the guesswork: every password is unique, long and pattern-free, so a leak on one site cannot unlock your email, bank or social accounts. Pair generated passwords with a password manager and two-factor authentication (2FA) for the strongest practical protection — and never share, reuse or write down a password in plain text.
